I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Environmental Science and Ecological Engineering at **[Korea University](https://www.korea.edu/sites/en/index.do)** in Seoul, South Korea.

My research interests encompass trustworthy scientific AI, process-informed machine learning and deep learning, eco-hydroinformatics, and hybrid environmental modeling to understand and predict hydro-environmental processes and develop transferable, reliable, interpretable, and decision-relevant models for resilient freshwater ecosystems and water security.

My contributions span two complementary directions: advancing the understanding and modeling of stream–aquifer interactions, and developing uncertainty-aware AI frameworks for environmental and hydrological prediction and decision-making.


Before joining Korea University, I received my Ph.D. from **[The University of Science and Technology (UST)](https://www.ust.ac.kr/eng.do)** in Daejeon, where I concurrently worked as a **UST Student Research Fellow** at the **[Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT)](https://www.kict.re.kr/eng/)** in Goyang, South Korea.

My doctoral research was supervised by [Prof. Il-Moon Chung](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QlAJjXwAAAAJ&hl=en) and conducted in close collaboration with [Dr. Sun Woo Chang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jPHfuCIAAAAJ&hl=en) at KICT. My research contributions include advancing integrated stream–aquifer interaction modeling and developing uncertainty-aware artificial intelligence and machine-learning methods for environmental and hydrological applications.
