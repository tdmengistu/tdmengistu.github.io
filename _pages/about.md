I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Environmental Science and Ecological Engineering at **Korea University** in Seoul, South Korea.

My research interests encompass trustworthy scientific AI, machine/deep learning, and hybrid environmental modeling to understand and predict hydro-environmental processes and develop transferable, reliable, interpretable, and decision-relevant models for sustainable water management.

Before joining Korea University, I received my Ph.D. from **the University of Science & Technology (UST)**, where I concurrently worked as a UST Student Research Fellow at the **Korea Institute of Civil Engineering & Building Technology (KICT)** in South Korea. I also received my MSc and BSc in Hydraulic and Water Resources Engineering from **Jimma University**, Ethiopia.

My contributions span two complementary directions: advancing integrated stream–aquifer interaction modeling and developing uncertainty-aware AI and machine-learning frameworks for adaptive water management.
